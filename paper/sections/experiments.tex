\section{Experiments and Results}
\label{sec:experiments}
We answered RQ1--RQ3 using data exported from live AWS WAF probing and an RFC decoding surrogate pilot. Scripts were executed in a Windows environment using standard data science libraries (scikit-learn, joblib).

\Needspace{8\baselineskip}
\subsection{Experimental Flow}
We followed a consistent workflow:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Start with SQLi seeds and remap IDs to the target format.
  \item Apply mutation rules and send GET requests to the endpoint.
  \item Classify responses as \textsf{bypass}, \textsf{blocked}, or \textsf{other}.
  \item Aggregate outcomes to compare family-level behavior.
\end{enumerate}

We focus on attempt-level counts (RQ1) from \texttt{aws\_probe\_summary.json}, family-level rates (RQ2) from the trailing slice of \texttt{aws\_probe\_results.jsonl}, and surrogate pilot accuracy (RQ3) from \texttt{defense\_eval.json}.

\Needspace{8\baselineskip}
\subsection{RQ1: Scale, Labels, and Unique Payloads}
The archived run reports $N=5{,}000$ variants tested, with $N_{\mathrm{bp}}=2{,}083$ bypasses, resulting in $\mathrm{BR}_{\mathrm{agg}}=41.7\%$ (Table~\ref{tab:dataset}). Among the trailing 5,000 requests, we observed only 19 distinct payload strings in the bypass set and 34 in the blocked set, with no overlap. This indicates that the search reused similar surface forms under different transform labels. Our workload targeted URL-visible slots with percent-encoding and dialect modifications, highlighting the necessity of extra probes for full-body inspection.

\Needspace{8\baselineskip}
\subsection{RQ2: Family-Level Heterogeneity}
Bypass success is highly concentrated. Figure~\ref{fig:bypass} and Table~\ref{tab:bypass_categories} show that the \emph{spawn\_identity}, \emph{spawn\_case\_mix}, and \emph{spawn\_null\_byte} cohorts achieved bypass rates near 100\%. Conversely, \emph{spawn\_encoding} and \emph{spawn\_hex\_style} were fully blocked ($\mathrm{BR}_f=0$). This pattern demonstrates that the WAF policy effectively handles nested encodings but struggles with specific structural mutations.

\Needspace{8\baselineskip}
\subsection{RQ3: Surrogate Accuracy and RFC Decoding}
We evaluated a TF-IDF logistic-regression surrogate on a 20-template HTTP pilot corpus (Table~\ref{tab:bypass_rates}). Test accuracy remained at 42.9\% for both raw and \texttt{unquote\_chain}-canonicalized strings. In this small sample, fixpoint percent-decoding neither improved nor degraded surrogate performance.

\Needspace{8\baselineskip}
\subsection{Threats to Validity and Reproducibility}
\textbf{Threats.} Our labels rely on script-specific heuristics. Results are bound to a specific policy snapshot and single-channel URL injection. Furthermore, the search distribution is explicitly non-uniform, reflecting operational biases. 
\textbf{Reproducibility.} Summaries can be regenerated with scripts in the repository. Executing \texttt{run\_probe.py} yields fresh logs, though counts will naturally drift as WAF policies evolve. Probing was conducted exclusively against authorized targets.
